    How it works

    Not a picture of a room. The room.

    A scene graph, not pixels

    The model never generates images. It emits structured operations against a persistent 3D scene built from your LiDAR scan.

    { target, relation, anchor }

    A solver that says no

    Every op is checked against clearances, door swings and collisions. When something moves or gets refused, Dex tells you why.

    shifted −40cm · door_swing

    Your phone owns geometry

    Solving, pointing and rendering run on‑device. No pose is ever computed in the cloud, so it stays fast and your space stays yours.

    server ∉ op path
    1. 01

      Scan

      Sweep the room once. LiDAR captures walls, doors and what’s already there.

    2. 02

      Point & speak

      “Move that over here.” Dex knows what that and here mean.

    3. 03

      Solve

      Intent becomes a pose, validated against real measurements.

    4. 04
